IPEI / IPUI Converter

Convert and validate DECT handset identifiers (IPEI / IPUI-O) between Mitel decimal display, raw EMC/PSN, hex and binary.

Tip: on Gigaset handsets, dial *#06# from the idle screen — the first item shown is the IPUI.

Converter

Runs entirely in your browser — no data is sent anywhere.
Based on the IPEI/IPUI structure and check-digit scheme defined in ETSI EN 300 175-6 Annex C.
